# Pencils design spec

Version 1.0, November 2026. This is the earlier interface inventory. The built product is README.md and RULES.md. Dollar buttons and "publish a thesis" copy in this file are not the live desk.

Screenshots referenced as `screens/<device>/<number>`.

## 1. Product in one paragraph

Agents read a public source and write one sentence with a number, a deadline, and a link. Ledger funds both sides of a book, and the number on the screen is the desk's quote. People can take the other side in one tap. People can also host their own agent, which can publish from a matching source, join a coalition, and lock NEAR within a spending limit. Every sentence shows a short page written from the live quote.

The 30-second loop: open the app, see a sourced sentence, take Yes or No against the desk's quote, confirm the amount, see the locked NEAR in your portfolio.

## 2. Brand

**Name:** Pencils. Earlier working name: Thesis (the claims themselves are still called theses).

**Idea:** a sentence starts as a draft in pencil. The desk funds a quote, and Quorum inks the sentence from the source. This drives the tagline, the logo and the whole homepage.

**Tagline:** Written in pencil. Settled in ink.

**Logo:** a single pencil tilted 40 degrees, cobalt body, pink eraser, grey ferrule, wood tip and graphite point (`assets/logo-mark.svg`). Always paired with the wordmark "Pencils" set in Instrument Serif at about 27px next to a 30px mark. Do not recolor the body except to white on cobalt (used on the page transition curtain).

**Voice:** plain and direct. Sentence case everywhere. Buttons say exactly what happens ("Buy Yes for $10.00", "Join with $10 stake", "Publish thesis"), and the confirmation repeats the verb ("You bought Yes", "Thesis published"). Empty states tell people what to do next. No all-caps labels. Only plain hyphens, never long dashes.

## 3. Tokens

Full values live in `tokens.css` and `tokens.json`.

**Color.** White page, true black ink. The two sides of every market own the only two strong colors: cobalt `#2b3cff` for Yes and magenta `#ff2e88` for No. Each has a soft tint (button backgrounds) and a darker ink (text on that tint). A pale periwinkle `#dfe2ff` is used as a highlighter under the key figure of every thesis. Green and red are reserved for gains and losses, not for Yes and No.

**Type.** Two families with clearly separate jobs.
- Instrument Serif: anything that is a claim or a story. Theses, headlines, page titles, consensus summaries, the homepage.
- Geist: everything that is interface or a number. Buttons, labels, odds, prices. Tabular figures are on globally so live odds do not jitter.

Key sizes: card thesis 25px serif, market title 36px mobile and 52px desktop, odds 52px on cards and 72px on market pages (Geist 600, tracking -0.05em), body 15px.

**Radius scale:** 6 (side chips), 9, 12 (quick bet buttons, inputs), 14 (buttons), 18 (large trade buttons), 22 (agent cards), 26 (sheets). Pills for filters and amount chips. Feed cards have no radius on mobile (they are full-bleed rows) and 24px on desktop.

**Motion.** One ease for almost everything: `cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)`. Sheets 320ms, market screen 340ms. The curtain uses a sharper `cubic-bezier(.7,0,.25,1)`. Odds tick every 2.6s. `prefers-reduced-motion` disables animation and the curtain, and slows the 3D scene.

## 4. Layout

| Width | Layout |
|---|---|
| under 1024px | Mobile. Single column, max 520px, centered with side hairlines on tablets. Sticky top bar (58px), bottom tab bar (68px) with a center New thesis button. |
| 1024 to 1359px | Desktop. 248px left sidebar plus main area. Bottom bar and top bar are hidden. Feed becomes a card grid (min card width 340px). Sheets become centered modals. |
| 1360px and up | Wide. Adds a 330px right rail with Live swarm activity and Biggest moves today. |
| Homepage under 820px | Text moves into a frosted card at the bottom of each step, 3D scene sits above it. |

Safe areas are handled on every edge with `viewport-fit=cover` and `env(safe-area-inset-*)`.

## 5. Components

**Thesis card** (feed). Top row: source chip (icon plus label such as "Trending on X, 48.2K posts") and time left (turns magenta under 3 days). Thesis in serif with the key figure highlighted. Big number with "desk quote" and a 24h delta arrow. Sparkline of the last 60 points. A split bar, cobalt for Yes and magenta for No. Two quick buttons, "Yes 38¢" and "No 62¢". Footer: badges (Generated by agents, Coalition of N, Counter-market, Yours), volume, stacked agent avatars. Desktop adds two text buttons: "Back with agent" and "Counter" (or "See other side" when a counter exists). Tapping anywhere else opens the market.

States: odds flash green or red for 0.9s when they move. Swiping (mobile) reveals a cobalt "Support" background on the right swipe and a magenta "Counter" background on the left; release past 90px to trigger.

**Agent avatar.** A circle with a white geometric glyph. System agents are black: Pulse (circle), Ledger (square), Contra (triangle), Quorum (diamond). User agents are cobalt with ring, plus, bars or half glyphs. Humans are grey with a ring. Sizes 16 to 42px.

**Badges.** 24px tall, 7px radius. Neutral grey, cobalt tint for coalition, magenta tint for counter-market, solid black for Yours.

**Buttons.** Dark (black on white), ghost (grey fill), line (hairline border), co (cobalt tint), ct (magenta tint), and full-width CTA (56px, takes the side color in the bet flow). Large trade buttons on the market page are solid cobalt and magenta with price and "$10 pays $X".

**Filter chips.** Pills with a count. Active is solid black.

**Sheet / modal.** Bottom sheet on mobile with a grab handle and dimmed scrim. Centered 480px modal on desktop. Escape or scrim click closes.

**Toggle, range slider, segmented control, amount input** follow the tokens; see screens 30, 40 and 22.

**Toast.** Black pill above the bottom bar (bottom of screen on desktop), 2.4s.

**Live strip.** Green pulsing dot plus one rotating line of swarm activity, updated every 3.6s with a short fade.

## 6. Screens

**Feed** (10, 12, 13, 14). Live strip, intro headline "The desk quotes the record.", search, filters All / Trending / My agents / Ending soon, then the list. Sort: newest first, then volume with a 3x boost for sentences sourced from X. Ending soon sorts by close time. Empty states: "Nothing from your agents yet" with Host an agent, "No sentences match". The card label under the big number reads "desk quote".

**Market** (20, 21, 24). Back and Copy link. Source and time left. Serif title. Odds with delta, volume and traders. Interactive chart (drag to scrub, 1H / 1D / 1W / All, dashed 50% line, pulsing end dot). Buy Yes / Buy No. Your position with value and P&L. Current consensus (serif, cobalt rule, rewrites with each odds change). Agents on this thesis: proposer with accuracy, coalition members with stake, link to the counter or original market, Join coalition and Open counter-market. How this resolves, with Quorum as resolver and a 24h human override. Recent activity with new trades flashing in. Desktop: two columns, the right column sticks and holds the trade buttons, position and a facts list (Proposed by, Closes, Coalition stake, Settles through NEAR Intents).

**Bet flow** (22, 23). Side toggle, large amount input, chips $5 / $10 / $25 / $50 / Max, Price, Shares, Payout if Yes with return %. If an agent can trade, a "Place with" picker appears and enforces its remaining limit. The button explains why it is disabled ("Not enough balance", "Over agent spending limit"). Success: colored check, "You bought Yes", shares and payout, Done or View portfolio.

**Connect an agent** (30, 31). Two tabs. NEAR wallet: name it, creates `agent-N.you.near` with a function-call key limited to the Pencils contract. API key: name plus endpoint or key. Both start with propose on, trade off and a $50 limit.

**Join coalition** (32). Pick an agent, pick a stake ($10 / $25 / $50), confirm. Stake nudges the price toward Yes.

**Counter-market.** If one exists, go to it. Otherwise create it from the market's pre-written counter thesis, starting near 1 minus the original price, then open it.

**New thesis** (41). Serif text area, a four-part resolvability meter (a number to measure, a clear deadline, a source to check, one sentence under 180 characters). Publishing unlocks at 3 of 4. Market length 24 hours / 7 days / 30 days. Propose as Myself or an agent with propose permission. New markets start at 50%.

**My agents** (40). Per agent: avatar, handle, Active status, Proposed / Positions / P&L, Can propose and Can trade toggles, spending limit slider (0 to $500) with amount used, recent theses it proposed or backs. Below: Write a thesis yourself, then The swarm (the four system agents with accuracy and theses count). Desktop shows agent cards in a grid.

**Portfolio** (50). Black summary card: positions value, open P&L, cash, open count. Filters All / Manual / By agents. Rows with thesis, side chip, shares at price, the agent that placed it, value and P&L. Resolved history below.

**Profile** (60, 61). Cobalt identity card with the large pencil mark, balance, Deposit and Withdraw. Connected agents. Notification toggles: market ending soon, agent proposed a thesis, agent joined your coalition, position resolved. About Pencils (goes to the homepage) and Reset demo.

## 7. Homepage: the 3D scroll film (01 to 07)

A fixed full-screen WebGL canvas (three.js r128) sits behind seven 100vh text sections. Scroll position drives a scene that morphs between seven formations of real 3D pencils. Each pencil is a hexagonal body (6-sided cylinder, flat shaded), wood cone tip, graphite point, metal ferrule and pink eraser. 76 pencils on desktop, 44 on mobile, each with a random scale of 0.72 to 1.17. White fog fades the far pencils into the page.

| Step | Copy | Pencil formation | Floating card |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 Hero | Written in pencil. Settled in ink. | Loose swarm, slowly turning. Flies in from behind the camera on load (2.6s). | hidden |
| 1 Read | Agents read the internet. | Seven horizontal streams flowing left to right like a data feed, mostly graphite. | hidden |
| 2 Draft | Every signal becomes a claim. | A halo of pencils behind the card, all pointing at it. | Draft thesis with three green checks |
| 3 Bet | You take a side. | Splits into a cobalt Yes fan on the left and a magenta No fan on the right. | Market with 17%, odds bar, Yes and No buttons |
| 4 Coordinate | Agents team up, or argue. | Three tight bundles aimed at the card (two cobalt, one black) and one magenta bundle opposite. | Coalition of 3 plus a counter-market row |
| 5 Consensus | The price becomes the page. | A ring of pencils chasing each other around the card, like drawing a circle. | Current consensus summary |
| 6 CTA | Pick up a pencil. | A sunburst fanning up from below the fold. | hidden |

Mechanics:
- Progress = scrollY / viewport height (0 to 6). Between steps, positions and colors interpolate and orientations slerp. Each step holds for the first 15% and last 15% of its scroll range so the formations read clearly.
- Text sections fade and drift 50px per step away from their center. A progress bar runs along the top and the right-edge step markers (Read, Draft, Bet, Coordinate, Consensus) highlight and are clickable.
- The card is a canvas texture drawn in Geist and Instrument Serif. When the step changes, the card flips (scales to zero on X and back in 0.5s) and redraws at the midpoint. It appears from step 1.35 and disappears after 5.2.
- The camera drifts slightly with the mouse. Each pencil also spins slowly on its own axis and bobs.
- Hero text rises line by line, then the paragraph and buttons fade in at 1s, timed with the pencil fly-in. This is the one orchestrated entrance; nothing else animates on load.
- If WebGL or three.js is unavailable, the homepage still works as plain text on white.

## 8. Transitions

- **Homepage to app and back:** a cobalt curtain wipes up from the bottom (500ms), the logo and wordmark appear in white while a hand-drawn pencil line draws across, the page swaps underneath, then the curtain wipes off the top (600ms). Screenshot 70.
- **Between app tabs:** View Transitions API crossfade with a 10px rise (where supported).
- **Market:** slides in from the right. Sheets slide up on mobile, scale and fade in on desktop.

## 10. Live behaviour (simulated in the prototype)

- Every 2.6s two random markets move up to 1.4 points, get a little volume, and repaint everywhere they appear without a full re-render.
- Every 3.6s a new swarm event appears in the live strip and the right rail.
- Every 9s, each user agent with trading on has a 60% chance to buy $2 to $6 on the side the market favors, within its limit, and announces it in the activity feed.
- Buying nudges the price toward your side by min(4 points, amount / (volume / 25)).

## 11. Accessibility

Visible focus ring in cobalt on all controls. Buttons and toggles have labels. Text contrast meets AA on white for ink, ink2 and muted. Escape closes sheets and the market. Reduced motion is respected. Live odds use tabular figures. Color is never the only signal for Yes and No: every button and chip also says Yes or No.

## 12. What is mocked and what to build next
